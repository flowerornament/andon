# Budget

A goal states a **budget**: the finite effort it may spend, in agent-hours,
hours, days or runs, in its `## Budget` section. The board shows it as
spent/allowed (`1.5/4 ah`) and flags `over budget on <goal>`.

The founding design used Shape Up's word for this. A new user met it
undefined in `board --help`, the board column, the hook line, the flags,
`check goal` and the required goal section. "Budget" says what andon
enforces: an amount a goal can overspend (andon-rhy).

The rename is mechanical and keeps no alias: identifiers, ledger attributes
(`budget`), JSON fields (a goal's `budget` as written, its `amount` and
`unit`, and `used`), the config keys `goal.budget` and `board.budget_unit`,
and the required section. bd's goals were rewritten to `## Budget`, so
nothing parses the old name.

This is the one place the corpus was edited rather than appended to: the
earlier dated documents use the new word throughout, by the coordinator's
ruling, so a reader never meets two names for one thing.
